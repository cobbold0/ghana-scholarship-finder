# Ghana Scholarship Finder — Monetization Strategy

## Primary Revenue Model

The initial business model is free scholarship discovery supported by advertising. Build a useful, trustworthy platform before optimizing revenue.

## Advertising

Potential provider: Google AdSense. Do not assume an account exists or has been approved. Document any required owner action in `NEXT_STEPS.md`.

### Suitable Ad Locations

Homepage, informational guides, category pages, scholarship listing pages (where appropriate), other public content pages.

### Avoid Ads

- Over scholarship details
- Beside application links in a misleading way
- Where they resemble official scholarship content
- In a way that causes accidental clicks
- In a way that obscures deadlines or eligibility

The official application link must remain easy to identify.

## Free Features

Browse, search, filter, view eligibility, view deadlines, access official application links, save scholarships locally. Do not charge students to see basic opportunity information.

## Future Revenue Options

- **Sponsored listings** — only when clearly labelled; never presented as independently verified recommendations.
- **Affiliate links** — test preparation, language courses, educational resources, application support services, career development. Clearly disclose affiliate relationships.
- **Premium tools** — deadline planner, application tracker, document checklist, personalized alerts, progress dashboard. Do not require premium access for basic scholarship discovery.

## Analytics

Measure usage without collecting unnecessary personal data (scholarship viewed, search performed, filter applied, official application link clicked, scholarship saved). Do not send users' personal documents or sensitive information to analytics providers.

## Monetization Principle

Trust and usefulness come first. Do not let advertising or sponsorship compromise the accuracy or clarity of scholarship information.
