# Ghana Scholarship Finder — SEO Strategy

## Objective

Attract students searching for scholarships available to Ghanaians. The initial focus is Ghana, with room to expand to other African countries.

## Search Intent

- **General:** scholarships in Ghana; scholarships for Ghanaian students; Ghana scholarship opportunities; scholarships for African students; international scholarships for Ghanaians
- **Education level:** undergraduate / masters / PhD scholarships for Ghanaians; scholarships for SHS graduates
- **Funding type:** fully funded scholarships for Ghanaians; international scholarships with full funding; partial scholarships for Ghanaian students
- **Informational:** how to apply for scholarships in Ghana; scholarship application requirements; how to write a scholarship personal statement; documents needed for scholarship applications

## Core Pages

```
/
/scholarships
/scholarships/[slug]
/categories
/categories/undergraduate
/categories/postgraduate
/categories/fully-funded
/guides
/guides/how-to-apply
/guides/scholarship-application-documents
/guides/personal-statement
```

Only publish category pages when there is enough relevant content.

## Scholarship Detail Pages

Unique title, concise meta description, canonical URL, clear H1, eligibility, deadline and status, official application link, provider, source and verification date when available. Do not index empty, duplicate, or unverified pages that offer no meaningful value.

## Informational Content

Create useful guides that answer actual student questions. Avoid generic filler and keyword stuffing.

## Internal Linking

Guides → categories; categories → listings; listings → detail pages; detail pages → relevant application guides.

## Structured Data

Use structured data only when it accurately describes the page. Do not fabricate ratings, reviews, authors, or scholarship details.

## Freshness

Display verification dates where available. Review expired opportunities and avoid presenting them as open. Do not automatically update deadlines or status without verification.

## Sitemap and Robots

Include useful public pages in the sitemap. Exclude private or user-specific pages. Ensure robots.txt does not accidentally block the entire website.

## Performance

Mobile-first design, fast loading, optimized images, semantic HTML, minimal unnecessary JavaScript.

## SEO Principle

Every indexed page should help a student make progress toward finding or applying for a real opportunity.
